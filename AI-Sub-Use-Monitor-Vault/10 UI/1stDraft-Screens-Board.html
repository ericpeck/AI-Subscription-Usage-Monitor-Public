Information

Usage Ledger · 0.4.2 beta

What is this app?

A single place to see how much of each AI subscription you have used, and when the window resets. It reads your own usage page on each provider with the session you signed in with — nothing leaves the phone, and there is no account to make here.

Design philosophy

One question per screen: am I near my limit? So the app is set like a newspaper, not a dashboard — a figure you can read at arm's length, a bar for how much you have spent, and a hairline for how much of the window has elapsed. Fill ahead of the hairline means you are burning it faster than the clock. No charts on the front, no metrics you did not ask for.

Frequently asked

Why do I sign in inside the app?

The session has to stay attached to the app to read your usage. An external browser hands the session to itself, not to us.

Where is my data kept?

On this phone, in the app's own storage. No server of ours ever sees it. Removing an account deletes its session and figures.

Can I add the same platform twice?

Yes — tick Work before adding and it becomes a separate tab, marked with a gold W.

Why are my numbers a few minutes behind?

Figures refresh on the cadence you set, and on tapping refresh. Providers also round their own counters.

A platform I pay for is missing.

Send it in — platforms are added as their usage pages become readable.

Donate to support

No ads, no subscription, no analytics. If it saves you a monthly overage, it has paid for itself — anything you send covers the developer account and the odd late night.
