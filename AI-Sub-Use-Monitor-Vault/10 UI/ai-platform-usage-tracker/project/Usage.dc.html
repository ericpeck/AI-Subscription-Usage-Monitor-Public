Am I near my limit?

One screen per subscription, following the shape of the app you showed me: a window, how much of it you have used, how much of the window is gone, and when it resets. Nothing else — charts come later.

What changed from v1

  • No spend, no head-to-head, no trends. Subscription limits only.
  • Tabs are platforms, exactly as in your reference — no dashboard above them.
  • Every window shows usage as the fill and time elapsed as a hairline. Fill ahead of the hairline means you are burning it faster than the clock.
  • Light paper instead of the dark reference: the design system has no dark surfaces.
  • Each platform carries its own hue — Claude coral, Gemini blue, ChatGPT green, Grok black, Cursor graphite — muted to the system's press register, with a deeper step for the display figure so it stays legible on paper.

Live here

  • Tap a window for the plain-English read on pace
  • Gear opens settings: which subscriptions you hold, refresh cadence
