Usage Ledger

31 August 2026Nine platformsPhone and watch

One screen per subscription, answering one question: am I near my limit? Every window shows two things — the fill is how much you have used, the hairline is how much of the window has elapsed. Fill ahead of the hairline means you are burning it faster than the clock.

This is the screen record for the phone app and its Wear OS companion. Each screen carries the id it has on the design board, so a note against "4f" or "3g" points at one thing.

Adding an Account

Linear, one account at a time, and one account per subscription. No tabs exist until something is added; afterwards the plus repeats the flow for the next platform — never the same one twice.

1a Nothing connected
1b Pick one, tick Work
1d Sign in, in the app
1e It becomes a tab

One Account per Subscription

A subscription's sign-in holds one session at a time, so a platform connects once — not once for work and once for personal. The add list carries that state: a connected platform loses its Work tick and its plus and reads Active, naming the tab it already owns. When a cookie expires the tab stays in place and becomes a sign-in; the add list shows the platform as Expired with a Log in again button that only walks you to that tab. Nothing is ever added twice, and re-authorising happens where the account already lives. 1f replaces 1b.

1f Active — already a tab
1g Expired — log in again
1h The tab signs in again

Wear OS

The bar becomes an arc and the hairline a tick on it. Wear is dark-theme only, so every face is black with the platform hues lifted to their lighter, less saturated steps. Faces are drawn at the real 454px display inside the 539px skin.

3g Nothing connected
3a Two windows, two arcs
3c Reset notification
3f Tile — three sessions
3e Tile — one per card

Tiles are ProtoLayout, not Compose — one screen, no scrolling, reached by swiping from the watch face. Both follow PrimaryLayout: title slot, main slot, one action below.

Build notes

The phone screens are Compose with Material 3 — tabs scroll, the plus stays pinned. The watch app is Wear Compose, but the two tiles are ProtoLayout, a declarative subset where arcs are ArcLine rather than a Composable.

The supplied skin is a 454px round AVD: point an emulator at it to preview at true size. Hand over this record and the assets rather than the board's markup — Compose is a different layout model, and a port fights it.
